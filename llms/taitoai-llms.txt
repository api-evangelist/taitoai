# Taito.ai

> Taito.ai is a people operations system that automates time-off, attendance, documents, and performance, so a small team can support a growing one. Built for teams scaling from 30 to 300 employees. ISO 27001 certified and GDPR compliant. Pricing is one tier, per-seat, with both the people-ops and performance modules included.

## Product

- [Overview](https://taito.ai/): Run people ops on autopilot — onboarding, time off, reviews, and compliance handled in one product.
- [People directory](https://taito.ai/people-directory): Single source of truth for employee data, org chart, and custom records.
- [Time off & attendance](https://taito.ai/time-off-attendance): Multi-region time-off and attendance policies with pre-payroll reports.
- [Documents](https://taito.ai/documents): Document workflows and eSignatures with retention controls.
- [Performance](https://taito.ai/performance): Performance cycles, calibration, and AI-assisted attributes — included in the base plan.
- [AI / People agents](https://taito.ai/agents): Slack agent, MCP integration, and no-code workflows over your people data.

## Solutions

- [For founders](https://taito.ai/founders): Scale people ops without scaling the people-ops team.
- [For people leaders](https://taito.ai/people-leaders): Run feedback and performance without spreadsheets or stitched tools.
- [For operators](https://taito.ai/operators): One system for the whole employee lifecycle — audit-ready records, scheduled reports, regional compliance.

## Pricing

- [Pricing](https://taito.ai/pricing): €10/seat/month monthly, €8/seat/month annual. One tier — people-ops and performance modules included, no add-ons. Not an early-adopter discount; this is the price.
